 Campaign intelligence - Marketing Campaign Analysis
 
 Project Overview 
 Campaign intelligence is a power bi project focused on analyzing customer demographics, purchasing behavior, spending patterns and marketing campaign responses.
 The report provides insights into custumer characteristics, purchasing preferences and campaign acceptance.
The report is Structured into three analytical pages: Campaign overview, Customer insights and Campaign effectiveness.

Business Objective
 The objective of this project was to explore customer data, understand purchasing patterns and examine campaign response behavior to identify insights that could support marketing decision making.
 
Dataset Overview
- Total customer records;2240
- Missing income values;24
- Duplicate records;non identified during data preparation.
- Campaign acceptance;334 customers
- Campaign acceptance rate;14.9%
Tools used 
- Power BI desktop
- Power query 
- Data visualization 
- Dashboard design
Dashboard pages
1. Campaign overview
This page provides a summary of customer activity spending and purchasing channels.
Key metrics:
- Total customer records;2240
- Average customer income: Ksh 52,247
- Total spending: Ksh 1,356,988
- Campaign acceptance rate;14.9%
Visuals:
- Spending by product category
- Purchases by channel
- Comparing response distribution
2. Customer insights
this project explores customer demographics income distribution and spending patterns.
Key metrics:
- Avarage customer age: 56
- Average customer income: ksh 52,247
- Average spending for customer: Ksh 606
Visuals:
- Customer Age distribution
- Customer income distribution
- Total spending by educational level
- Total spending by maritual status
3. Campaign effectiveness
This page examines campaign acceptance and explodes acceptance patterns across customer groups.
Key metrics:
- Campaign acceptance rate; 14.9%
- Customers who accepted 334
- Total customers 2240.
- Total spending ksh 1.356,988
Visuals:
- Campaign acceptance comparison
- Campaign response distribution
- Total spending by campaign response
- Campaign acceptance rate by education
Key findings:-
- 334 out of 2240 customers accepted the campaign representing a response rate of approximately 14.9%
- Wine was the highest spending product category in the report.
- Star purchases represented the largest purchase channel in the overview.
- PhD customers recorded the highest campaign acceptance rate among the education groups displayed.
- Customer spending and campaign acceptance patterns varied across demographic groups.
